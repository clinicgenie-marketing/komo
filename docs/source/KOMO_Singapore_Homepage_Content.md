## Before you visit

### How do I choose a treatment?

Start with what you’d like from your visit, whether that’s focused scalp care, conditioning for your hair or a relaxing head spa. If you’re unsure, speak with our team before booking.

### What happens during the scalp analysis?

Scalp imaging gives our team a closer view of your scalp and hair. A consultant explains the observations and discusses how they inform your care options.

### Can I visit if I don’t have a particular scalp concern?

Yes. You can explore our head spa treatments simply as a way to enjoy scalp and hair care while taking time to relax.

### How much time should I allow?

Appointment time depends on your selected treatment. Ask our team to confirm the total duration, including consultation and hair finishing, when booking.

### What if I have a sensitive scalp or product allergies?

Let our team know before your appointment so we can discuss the products and treatment steps with you.

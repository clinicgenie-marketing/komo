## What brings you in?

**My roots feel oily, but my ends feel dry.** Those are two different parts of your hair routine. Start with Signature Scalp Care for a scalp cleanse. If it’s your lengths that feel rough when you comb them, explore conditioning and hair care. **Explore Scalp Concerns** · **Explore Hair Care**

**I’m seeing more hair in my brush.** You can look at the area that concerns you with a KOMO consultant and discuss how you care for your scalp. Finding the cause of hair loss calls for a medical assessment. **Read About Hair Fall and Thinning**

**I’d like a head massage.** Choose Waterfall for a rhythmic scalp massage, or explore Meridian if you enjoy focused pressure and gua sha. **Compare the Head Spas**

<!-- Section 05 · What happens on a first visit? -->

## What happens on a first visit?
<!-- Toggle labels · Mirror / Basin -->


**At the mirror.** Part your hair where you’ve noticed a change. It’s easier to show your consultant the spot than to find the right words for it. Scalp imaging gives you both a close view of that area while you talk through cosmetic care.

**At the basin.** For Meridian, that means massage and gua sha. For a scalp care visit, the conversation turns to cleansing and the product selected for your scalp. If you have an ingredient in mind, this is a good moment to see the bottle.

**How Scalp Imaging Works** · **Read The Straits Times Feature on the Orchard Scan**

## First visit questions


**Does the treatment time include my whole visit?** The times listed above refer to the treatment. For planning your day, use the single visit enquiry to check arrival and finish times with Orchard.

**Can I come for a single session?** Single visit and trial conditions need to be checked for the session you choose. The quote request asks about package requirements and optional extras before you commit to a booking.
